## What is a site cabin?

A site cabin is a portable, prefabricated structure used on construction sites as temporary office space, storage, or accommodation for workers. It provides a secure, convenient workspace or shelter in remote or temporary locations.

## What size are site cabins?

Site cabins come in various sizes, typically ranging from 10 to 40 feet in length, depending on the requirements for workspace, storage, or accommodation.

## What size is a site container office?

A site container office usually ranges from 20 to 40 feet in length and offers sufficient space for office furniture, equipment, and staff, making it a flexible option for on-site administration.

## What are the dimensions of a 20ft site cabin?

A 20ft site cabin typically measures around 20 feet in length, 8 feet in width, and 8 feet in height, providing a compact yet functional space for office or storage needs on a worksite.
