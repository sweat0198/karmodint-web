## A moulded polyester shell, built to stand outside

The GRP Cabin is a modular cabin moulded from glass reinforced polyester, and the material is what
the range is built around. Polyester holds up against sun, rain and wind, which is what gives the
cabin its working life outdoors, and its insulating properties keep the interior comfortable in both
summer and winter.

The shell is light for its size. That makes a cabin straightforward to transport and to relocate if
the site plan changes later — useful on construction sites and event grounds, where a control point
rarely stays in one place for the life of the project.

## Delivered ready for use

Cabins arrive complete, so putting one into service is a matter of siting the unit rather than
building it. The same holds if it needs to move again.

## Five sizes, from a single pitch to a shared control room

The range runs from 1.50m × 1.50m up to 2.70m × 2.70m. The smaller sizes suit a confined pitch where
one person needs shelter and a clear view of the approach. The wider models give enough floor area
for more than one member of staff, and enough room to bring in additional equipment or furniture
where the role calls for it.

## Where GRP Cabins are used

- **Security and control points** at site, factory and residential entrances.
- **Car parks and valet services**, where staff need shelter without losing sight of the approach.
- **Construction sites**, as a temporary management office or observation booth.
- **Events and organisations**, as a ticket control or management cabin.
- **Sales and service kiosks** — food and drink counters, bread distribution points, and newspaper
  or small retail stands.
