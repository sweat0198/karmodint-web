# About Hero Business Campus Design

## Goal

Replace the current generic About hero building with an attractive photorealistic concept visualisation of a large, active modular business and education campus.

## Scene

- Two-storey beige-and-white modular buildings frame a landscaped courtyard.
- Architecture preserves catalogue language: visible modular frame rhythm, white insulated wall panels, practical doors and windows, and believable connected volumes.
- Workers and students cross the plaza, sit together, and use bicycle parking.
- Café-style outdoor seating, mature trees, planted edges, and clean paving make the campus feel complete and established.
- Warm bright afternoon light creates welcoming, ambitious tone without looking cinematic or artificial.

## Composition

- Square or near-square source suits existing `h-80 sm:h-96` hero card across mobile and desktop.
- Main building and busiest courtyard activity stay inside central crop-safe area.
- Eye-level or gently elevated architectural viewpoint shows scale without drone distortion.
- People remain secondary; architecture stays hero subject.

## Truthfulness

- Scene represents imagined design capability, not delivered client project.
- Add visible `Concept visualisation` disclosure inside image card.
- Alt text identifies concept visualisation without claiming headquarters, ownership, location, or delivery.
- Avoid signage, client names, project names, logos, readable text, branded uniforms, and identifiable institutions.

## Constraints

- No construction state, cranes, machinery, mud, fencing, floating modules, implausible cantilevers, or conventional concrete/glass tower language.
- No duplicated people, malformed bicycles, distorted hands, or faces prominent enough to distract.
- Preserve calm campus character despite active population.

## Delivery

- Save versioned PNG master and optimized WebP under `public/images/about/`.
- Keep existing About hero copy and layout.
- Remove scene-wide hover zoom; static photography avoids crop loss and unnecessary motion.
- Use eager loading and high fetch priority because image sits above fold.
- Add component coverage for paths, disclosure, truthful alt text, loading priority, sizing, and static presentation.
