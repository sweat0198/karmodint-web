# Solution Cover Imagery Design

**Date:** 2026-09-22

**Status:** Approved

## Goal

Create one new review-only cover image for each of the 12 published Solution entries. Images must present real Karmod product forms in attractive, feasible UK project settings.

## Chosen Direction

Use grounded composite photography. Each generated image receives two kinds of evidence:

1. A catalogue render controls product geometry, materials, openings, frame details, and proportions.
2. A matching Gallery project photo controls installation context, groundworks, surrounding architecture, scale, and photographic realism.

Existing generated Solution images are excluded from all references.

## Visual System

- Photorealistic commercial project photography.
- Wide landscape composition, composed for both 16:9 and centred 21:9 crops.
- Product occupies the primary focal area and remains readable at card size.
- Soft UK daylight, restrained grading, realistic material texture, and credible wear.
- British site practice, road markings, kerbs, temporary fencing, planting, architecture, and weather establish the target market.
- Sparse people only when needed for scale; no identifiable faces.
- No customer branding, invented logos, promotional text, watermarks, fake certification marks, or implausible luxury finishes.
- No structural additions unsupported by Karmod products. Solution-specific fit-out remains feasible and visually secondary.

## Deliverables

- Twelve new versioned review assets under `public/images/solutions/review/`.
- Prompt and reference manifest under the same directory.
- Existing Solution images unchanged.
- No Sanity upload or document mutation.

## Review Standard

Reject an output when product family is ambiguous, openings or roof geometry drift, scale is implausible, access or foundations look unsafe, scene resembles a fantasy render, or text/logos become garbled. Keep only one strongest candidate per Solution.
