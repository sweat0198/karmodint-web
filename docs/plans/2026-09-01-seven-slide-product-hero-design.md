# Seven-Slide Product Hero Design

## Goal

Expand the homepage hero from five to seven slides. Keep the two approved modular-container scenes, then present five exact cabin sizes in distinct, credible UK deployments.

## Slide Order

1. 2.3 × 6 m Modular Container — British residential construction site.
2. 3 × 7 m Modular Container — modern UK commercial development.
3. 1.5 × 1.5 m GRP Cabin — leafy school or sports-ground entrance.
4. 1.35 × 2.1 m Panel Cabin — organised construction-site gate.
5. 2.15 × 2.65 m MetroCity Modular Cabin — premium residential development entrance.
6. 2.65 × 2.65 m KompoCity Composite Cabin — contemporary office or hotel forecourt.
7. 2 × 4 m Bulletproof Security Cabin — discreet critical-infrastructure checkpoint.

## Product Fidelity

Every new image uses the exact project-local front and left-diagonal product renders as references. Preserve dimensions, silhouette, materials, colours, window and door placement, exterior lighting, roof treatment, and model-specific details. Bulletproof glazing, ports, floodlights, and armoured proportions must remain intact.

## Visual Language

Use natural architectural product photography with realistic British daylight, restrained props, temperate planting, plausible paving, and recognisably UK architecture. Product occupies the central crop-safe area of a 4:3 landscape frame. Keep lower-left and upper-right overlay zones quiet.

Avoid people, weapons, vehicles blocking products, fake branding, generated text, tropical vegetation, fantasy architecture, oversaturated HDR, and excessive CGI polish.

## Integration

Save five optimised WebP assets under `public/images/hero/`. Replace the three legacy combined-category slides with five size-specific slide objects. Keep existing autoplay, navigation, timing, transitions, overlays, and responsive image treatment unchanged.

## Verification

- Inspect all five generated assets against their reference renders.
- Confirm 4:3 landscape dimensions and efficient WebP sizes.
- Expand the hero component test from five to seven images.
- Verify all seven image paths, titles, subtitles, and alt text.
- Run focused tests, full tests, and production build.
