## Generation prompt

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: About page landscape hero image, 4:3 aspect ratio, for a UK modular building company.
Primary request: Create an attractive but MODEST and realistic imaginary UK modular installation. The earlier concept was too large; scale down significantly to the kind of small commercial project a growing supplier would actually deliver today.
Reference roles: Image 1 is our earlier imaginary campus, to retain only the appealing photographic lighting and charcoal/off-white product palette. It is TOO LARGE: replace its two-storey building, long wing, big plaza and crowd. Image 2 is a real Metro City office product, use its compact practical proportions, flat roof fascia, panel construction and window detailing. Image 3 is a real GRP kiosk, inspiration for manufactured booth shape only, no venue or branding.
Scene: a SINGLE small one-storey modular office/reception cabin, approximately 6 metres wide by 3 metres deep, installed beside a tidy paved parking area at a local British business park. Off-white panel walls with charcoal corner trims, a flat charcoal fascia, two standard office windows and one glazed entrance door; warm interior with a simple desk visible. One separate compact charcoal GRP coffee/visitor kiosk, approximately 2 by 2 metres, sits a little forward and to one side, its service window open; subtle muted red roof trim. Both products are modest, grounded, believable manufactured cabins, no extravagant architectural detailing. A couple of planters, low native hedge and two mature deciduous trees provide a welcoming setting. One small outdoor table with two chairs and just two adults chatting naturally near the kiosk. A low red-brick workshop softly visible in the background establishes UK context without competing with products.
Composition: eye-level three-quarter view, principal one-storey office and small kiosk clearly visible together in central 75% of frame; safe for near-square mobile crop. Both full roof lines and bases visible. Product fills frame, avoid excessive sky or empty pavement. Straight architectural verticals, natural 40mm-lens perspective.
Lighting: soft warm late-afternoon light after a light shower, subtle damp paving sheen, restrained greenery, fresh blue-grey sky. Beautiful natural architectural editorial photograph, realistic panel texture and glazing, calm confident composition. Welcoming and commercially credible.
Constraints: SMALL SCALE. Exactly one small single-storey office plus one compact booth. No second storey, external staircase, office wing, large complex, luxury campus, monumental glass entrance, large plaza, rows of cabins, construction camp, shipping freight container, crane or rubble. No logos, writing, signs, flags or watermark. No branding copied from references. Imaginary scene, not a claim of completed work. No CGI sheen, overdramatic sunset or oversaturation.
```
