# UK competitor research: garden rooms, cabins, gatehouses and specialist outdoor rooms

**Research date:** 2026-09-16  
**Geography:** United Kingdom  
**Sample:** 12 companies with first-party evidence of UK supply, delivery or installation  
**Evidence rule:** Company-owned websites only. Category names in quotation marks preserve the site's wording. Inferences are isolated and never counted as site-explicit categories.

## Scope and method

This is a specialist competitor slice for a wider Karmod solutions-page study. It covers businesses selling garden rooms, garden offices, log cabins, annexes, kiosks, gatehouses and related prefabricated outdoor rooms into the UK.

Selection required both:

1. A comparable physical product: a prefabricated, modular, panelised, flat-pack or workshop-built room/building.
2. Explicit UK-market evidence: UK manufacture, UK address, UK delivery, UK installation or a stated UK service area.

The study records two layers separately:

- **Site-explicit:** Product families and solution/use labels published by the company itself.
- **Analyst inference:** What the positioning implies for Karmod. These labels are not represented as competitor terminology.

Counts below measure page-taxonomy adoption in this 12-company purposive sample. They are not market share, sales volume or search demand.

## Executive findings

- **Use-led navigation is standard.** Ten of the 12 companies expose named use cases alongside product styles. Customers can shop for an outcome—office, gym, bar, studio, annexe—not only a construction type.
- **Office is the universal bridge category.** All 12 sites explicitly mention an office/workspace application, although CMT's use is a site-induction office rather than a domestic garden office.
- **Fitness and creative uses are nearly universal among lifestyle sellers.** Eleven of 12 explicitly mention gym/wellbeing and studio/hobby/creative uses; the security-cabin specialist is the exception.
- **Multi-use framing matters.** Green Retreats, Cabin Master, SMART, Outside In, Dunster House and others encourage combinations such as office + gym, office + bar or room + storage. This reduces dependence on a single lifestyle trend.
- **Annex/accommodation is a distinct high-spec branch.** Eight sites explicitly sell or discuss annexes, guest rooms, mobile homes, holiday lodges or other sleeping/living products. They separate these from ordinary rooms through Building Regulations, Caravan Act or habitable-spec language.
- **Commercial niches appear as explicit landing pages.** Education/school rooms, therapy/salon space, photography studios, Airbnb/living, ticketing/reception and gatehouse/access control all appear as named applications.
- **Delivery model signals product tier.** Lower-cost catalogues lean toward flat-pack/self-build; premium garden-room brands sell turnkey design-to-installation; specialist security cabins arrive fully assembled. Karmod should expose delivery state clearly on every solution page.
- **Security/gatehouse vocabulary is operational, not aspirational.** CMT uses “site entrance checkpoints”, “gatehouses”, “access control points”, “ticket office”, “reception booth” and “car park attendant kiosk”. These are stronger B2B landing-page labels than a generic “portable cabin”.

## Detailed source notes

### 1. Green Retreats

**Site-explicit taxonomy**

- The [Popular Uses page](https://www.greenretreats.co.uk/popular-uses/) groups room uses under Work From Home, Fitness, Entertainment, Extra Space, Education and Commercial. It exposes named children including Garden Offices, Home Business, Photography Studio, Garden Studio, Home Gym, Yoga Studio, Garden Bar, Man Cave, Games Room, Home Cinema, Outdoor Classrooms and Garden Storage.
- The [Garden Rooms page](https://www.greenretreats.co.uk/garden-rooms) separates style/specification ranges B-Range, G-Range, A-Range and X1 from “The Annex”, described as a full-time-living solution.
- The [comparison page](https://www.greenretreats.co.uk/compare/) explains which designs fit higher-headroom gym/golf simulator use and distinguishes permitted-development rooms from Building-Regulations/Caravan-Act annexes.

**Delivery and UK proof**

- Core products include professional installation. The [order process](https://www.greenretreats.co.uk/process/) says rooms use the company's truck fleet and trained staff; the comparison page identifies the B1 Kit exception.
- UK design/manufacture and Buckinghamshire facility are explicit on the Garden Rooms page.

**Inference**

- Separate **solution need** from **building range**. A gym is not a different structural catalogue; it is a path into appropriate models, sizes, headroom and finishes.

### 2. Cabin Master

**Site-explicit taxonomy**

- [Popular Uses](https://www.cabinmaster.co.uk/popular-uses) provides one of the clearest layered taxonomies: entertainment, work, fitness/wellbeing and extra space. Subcategories include Garden Bars, Garden Dining, Man Caves, Cinema Room, Home Business, Garden Offices, Photography Studio, Small Home Office, Office Pod, Beauty Room, Garden Gyms, Home Spa, Hobby & Craft Rooms and Music Rooms.
- [Our Buildings](https://www.cabinmaster.co.uk/our-buildings) adds product-led destinations: Micro Offices, BBQ Cabins, School Rooms and Camping Cabins & Lodges.

**Delivery and UK proof**

- The [Garden Rooms page](https://www.cabinmaster.co.uk/product/garden-rooms) states an end-to-end specification including base, build, insulation, internal electrics, flooring and painting, with 2–4 day on-site installation.
- Products are made to order in Derbyshire. The [work-from-home page](https://www.cabinmaster.co.uk/product/work-from-home-space) describes UK factories and delivery/installation.

**Inference**

- Reuse one product platform across residential, microbusiness, education and hospitality pages. Vary evidence and fit-out rather than duplicating the whole catalogue.

**Delivery and UK proof**

- The homepage says rooms are flat-packed on pallets and delivered to most of the UK. Buyers can self-assemble or buy turnkey design, planning, base, delivery, installation and electrics.

**Inference**

- “Therapy Rooms” and “Airbnb / Living” demonstrate commercial-intent long-tail pages. Both need proof tailored to their task: privacy, client comfort, waiting/storage for therapy; regulations, bathroom/kitchen and revenue case for accommodation.

- Own fleet covers over 95% of UK mainland. Unit is delivered fully assembled. Forklift/crane positioning and qualified final electrical connection remain customer/site requirements.

**Inference**

- Build distinct pages for Gatehouse, Security Checkpoint, Reception/Sign-in Kiosk, Ticket Office and Car Park Attendant Kiosk. Each page can reuse one product family while changing workflow proof, window/view requirements, access positioning and fit-out.
- Do not market it as a “safe room” without certification. CMT's own compatibility table explicitly rejects certified safe-room/fire-refuge use.

## Implications for Karmod's solution architecture

These are recommendations derived from the observed competitor patterns, not competitor claims.

### Recommended top-level solution clusters

1. **Work & Operations**
   - Garden Office
   - Site Office
   - Meeting Room
   - Reception / Sign-in Office
2. **Security & Access**
   - Gatehouse
   - Security Checkpoint
   - Car Park Attendant Kiosk
   - Ticket / Information Kiosk

   - Community Room

   - Secure Store
   - Workshop
   - Plant / Utility Room

### Page design requirements supported by this sample

- Put **solution name first**, then compatible products. Competitors overwhelmingly let buyers begin with “office”, “gym”, “annexe” or “gatehouse”.
- Keep **product family** as a second dimension: cabin type, wall system, delivery state, size and specification.
- Show **delivery state** in the hero or first specification band: flat-pack, panelised site build or fully assembled.
- Publish **multi-zone combinations** as first-class options. Outside In's DUO naming and several competitors' office/gym examples validate this.
- Tailor specifications by job. Examples: gatehouse visibility and lockset; gym floor and headroom; music-room acoustics; annexe utilities/regulations; therapy privacy and client experience; classroom occupancy and compliance.
- State **what is not included**: base, crane/forklift, utility trenching, final electrical connection, planning application and fit-out. Delivery ambiguity is a recurring friction point across this market.
- Use case studies as proof, tagged by both **solution** and **product model/size**. Green Retreats does this well in its filterable project gallery.

## Verification checklist before publication

- Recheck navigation labels immediately before using them in public comparative copy; competitor taxonomies and prices change.
- Do not publish “UK-wide” where a site says mainland UK, England only or charges regional surcharges.
- Do not treat permitted-development statements as universal legal advice.
- Do not equate “garden annexe” with independently habitable accommodation unless Building Regulations/Caravan Act language is explicit.
- Do not infer healthcare compliance from “therapy room” or industrial protection certification from “security cabin”.
- Do not compare headline prices without normalising foundation, delivery radius, installation, electrics, insulation, VAT and internal finish.
