# UK Market Naming: Containers, Portable Cabins, Gatehouses and Kiosks

**Research date:** 6 September 2026  
**Decision scope:** Customer-facing names for Karmod's current `Containers` and `Cabin` catalogue categories in the UK.

## Recommendation

Use this customer-facing pair:

| Current category | Recommended UK category name | Supporting descriptor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Containers** | **Portable Cabins** | Site offices, storage, accommodation and canteen units |
| **Cabin** | **Gatehouses & Kiosks** | GRP, insulated-panel and composite security/service buildings |

Do not rename the second category to **Kiosks** alone. That label fits ticketing, sales, vending and information uses, but underspecifies security posts, visitor reception and access control. **Gatehouses & Kiosks** covers the two main buyer jobs while retaining `security cabins`, `security booths`, `ticket kiosks` and related phrases in product copy and metadata.

Use **Portable Buildings** as a site-wide umbrella if a more sector-neutral label is needed. Use **Site Accommodation** as a construction-sector landing page or filter, not as the universal product category. Use **Modular Buildings** for joined, stacked or project-scale systems, not as the default label for every single unit.

The recommendation assumes the current `Containers` products are purpose-built occupied/storage units rather than conversions of ISO freight containers. If a product is made from an ISO shipping container, retain an explicit name such as **Container Office**, **Converted Shipping Container** or **Storage Container**.

## Why this fits UK buyer language

### 1. Portable cabin means a standalone, ready-to-use building

Portakabin divides its range into self-contained mobile buildings delivered ready for use and modular systems whose modules create larger buildings. It defines a portable cabin as a standalone steel-framed building and lists office, construction, storage, welfare and canteen applications. This closely matches Karmod's current `Containers` mix. [Portakabin product-range taxonomy](https://www.portakabin.com/gb-en/buildings/our-product-range/) and [portable-cabin definition](https://www.portakabin.com/gb-en/buildings/our-product-range/portable-cabins/).

Wernick also treats **Portable cabins** as the broad category for site offices, meeting rooms, welfare facilities, changing rooms, canteens, gatehouses, security booths, toilets, showers and storage. It lists **Storage & Shipping Containers** separately. [Wernick portable cabins](https://www.wernick.co.uk/hire/solutions/portable-cabins/).

Result: **Portable Cabins** is established UK trade language for Karmod's larger standalone units and avoids making every product sound freight-derived.

### 2. Container implies actual shipping/storage-container construction

Willbox describes its container offices as units built from ISO shipping containers and contrasts them with larger modular office buildings. [Willbox container offices](https://www.willbox.co.uk/product/offices).

Cleveland Containers makes the distinction explicit: its site-accommodation cabins are purpose-built anti-vandal units, not shipping containers; its container offices are converted shipping containers. [Cleveland Containers office comparison](https://www.clevelandcontainers.co.uk/news-and-blog/shipping-container-offices-the-complete-guide).

Algeco similarly separates secure storage containers from site offices, welfare units, site cabins and gatehouses within its site-accommodation range. [Algeco site accommodation](https://www.algeco.co.uk/temporary/site-accommodation-hire).

Result: generic **Containers** creates avoidable ambiguity unless the shell has genuine cargo-container lineage. The term remains useful when that construction is a selling point: high security, ISO form, stackability or conversion.

### 3. Gatehouse and security cabin express the compact unit's job

Algeco calls an access-control unit a **Portable Security Gatehouse** and says it can also serve as a ticket office, toll booth, weighbridge office or check-in office. [Algeco site accommodation](https://www.algeco.co.uk/temporary/site-accommodation-hire).

Wernick describes **Gatehouses** as compact secure units for entry control, security lodges and ticket booths. [Wernick portable cabins](https://www.wernick.co.uk/hire/solutions/portable-cabins/).

Willbox markets **Gatehouses & Security Cabins** for visitor reception, sign-in, barrier control, ticketing, car parks and delivery check-in. [Willbox gatehouses](https://www.willbox.co.uk/product/gatehouse).

Result: for the current small GRP/panel/composite range, **Gatehouse** and **Security Cabin** have clearer purchase intent than generic **Cabin**.

### 4. Kiosk works, but only with visible purpose

Glasdon's UK category deliberately combines **GRP Kiosks & Prefab Security Booths**. It uses kiosk for security gatehouses and sentry posts, while also listing ticket sales, vending and information-point applications. [Glasdon GRP kiosks and security booths](https://www.glasdon.com/uk/buildings-housings/grp-kiosks-prefab-booths).

Glasdon's model guidance further separates likely uses: one kiosk size is positioned as a security cabin or gatehouse; another as a ticket booth, vending kiosk or information point. [Glasdon Genesis kiosk guide](https://uk.glasdon.com/faq/complete-guide-to-genesis-grp-kiosks).

Another UK supplier places **Security Huts & Kiosks** and **Gatehouse Cabins** beneath the broader **Portable Buildings** category. [Cabins & Containers portable-building range](https://cabinsandcontainers.co.uk/portable-buildings/).

Result: **Kiosk** is legitimate UK supplier language for these compact buildings, especially GRP models, but it is an application label rather than a complete synonym for every cabin. Prefer **Ticket Kiosk**, **Retail Kiosk**, **Sales Kiosk**, **Information Kiosk** or **Vehicle Check-in Kiosk** at product/use-case level.

### 5. Site accommodation, welfare unit and modular building are narrower terms

Algeco uses **Site Accommodation** as the construction umbrella, then divides it into site offices, welfare units, anti-vandal units, fire-rated cabins, toilet/shower blocks, gatehouses and storage containers. [Algeco site accommodation](https://www.algeco.co.uk/temporary/site-accommodation-hire).

HSE defines construction welfare by facilities—namely toilets and washing, changing, eating and rest—not by the outer building type. [HSE welfare overview](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/healthrisks/welfare/). Wernick's **Welfare units** accordingly include toilets, canteen space, drying areas and sometimes office space. [Wernick welfare units](https://www.wernick.co.uk/hire/solutions/welfare/).

HSE's technical term is **temporary accommodation units (TAUs)** for units brought onto construction sites to provide offices, canteens and welfare facilities. It covers simple mobile units through multi-storey composite units. Useful compliance terminology; weak customer navigation language. [HSE HSG168, Appendix 1](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg168.pdf).

Algeco notes that *temporary building*, *cabin*, *module* and *unit* are often used interchangeably in construction, then adopts **temporary buildings** as its clarity term for prefabricated modular structures providing office, welfare or storage space. [Algeco JCoP 10.2 guide, page 7](https://www.algeco.co.uk/sites/default/files/2026-01/Algeco_JCoP10.2_Guide_0126.pdf).

Result:

- **Site Accommodation**: strong for construction campaigns and grouped site setups; too construction-specific for education, commercial, residential and public-facing uses.
- **Welfare Unit**: use only when the unit provides actual worker welfare facilities. A plain office or storage room is not a welfare unit.
- **Modular Building**: strongest for multi-module, joined/stacked or permanent-grade projects. A single relocatable unit can technically be modular, but the label raises expectations of a larger building system.
- **Temporary Accommodation Unit**: compliance language, not recommended navigation copy.

## Term-by-term buyer meaning

| Term | Likely UK buyer meaning | Fit for current `Containers` | Fit for current `Cabin` | Main ambiguity |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| **Portable Cabin** | Standalone relocatable room, often site-ready | **High** | Medium | Construction-site feel; broad function |
| **Portable Building** | Broad professional umbrella across sectors | High | High | Less specific purchase intent |
| **Site Accommodation** | Construction-site offices, welfare, toilets, canteens and related units | High for a sector page | Low | Sounds construction-only |
| **Portable Office / Site Office** | Workspace for a defined team or project | High for office products | Low | Function-specific, not a category umbrella |
| **Welfare Unit** | Toilets/washing/eating/rest/drying, sometimes office space | Medium for equipped products | Low | Incorrect for unequipped cabins |
| **Modular Building** | Joined/stacked scalable building or permanent-grade project | Medium | Low | Overstates small standalone products |
| **Container** | Shipping/storage box or an explicit conversion | Conditional | Low | Freight, storage and industrial appearance |
| **Security Cabin** | Staffed guard/control shelter | Low | **High** | Excludes retail/information uses |
| **Gatehouse** | Staffed entrance, visitor and vehicle-control point | Low | **High** | Excludes non-entrance kiosks |
| **Kiosk** | Small sales, ticket, vending, information or check-in point | Low | High with qualifier | Also associated with unstaffed digital terminals or open retail stands |
| **Booth / Hut** | Small single-purpose shelter | Low | Medium | Lower-value or informal tone |

## Recommended catalogue vocabulary

### Current `Containers` category

**Category:** Portable Cabins  
**Landing-page H1:** Portable Cabins & Site Accommodation  
**Optional broader navigation label:** Portable Buildings

Rename subcategories by buyer job:

| Current | Recommended |
| --- | --- |
| Office | **Site Offices** or **Portable Offices** |
| Depo | **Storage Units** |
| Living Area | **Accommodation Units** |
| Kiosk / Canteen | **Canteen & Catering Units** |

Avoid **Depo**. It is not the UK product noun. **Depot** means a storage/operating location; **Storage Unit**, **Site Store** or **Storage Container** names the product.

Use **Welfare Units** as a separate subcategory only when products include welfare functions such as toilets, washing, rest, canteen or drying facilities.

### Current `Cabin` category

**Category:** Gatehouses & Kiosks  
**Landing-page H1:** Security Gatehouses, Cabins & Kiosks  
**Technical descriptor in copy:** Portable GRP, insulated-panel and composite buildings

Keep material as a filter or range attribute. Lead product names with application where configuration supports it:

- **GRP Security Cabin & Kiosk**
- **Insulated Security Gatehouse**
- **MetroCity Kiosk & Gatehouse**
- **KompoCity Composite Gatehouse**

Where one shell serves several jobs, do not force a false single use. Use a combined name, then create application landing pages for **Security Cabins**, **Gatehouses**, **Ticket Kiosks**, **Retail Kiosks**, **Information Kiosks** and **Portable Offices**.

## Naming hierarchy

```text
Portable Buildings
├── Portable Cabins                         current Containers
│   ├── Site Offices
│   ├── Storage Units
│   ├── Accommodation Units
│   └── Canteen & Catering Units
└── Gatehouses & Kiosks                     current Cabin
    ├── Security Cabins & Gatehouses        application
    ├── Ticket, Retail & Information Kiosks application
    └── GRP / Panel / Composite             material filters or ranges
```

Keep **Modular Buildings** alongside this tree only for project-scale linked or stacked systems. Keep **Shipping Containers** alongside it only if Karmod sells actual freight/storage units or freight-container conversions.

## Implementation note

This is a naming recommendation, not a URL-migration plan. If adopted, change visible category labels first. Preserve existing `/containers` and `/cabin` URLs until a mapped redirect and internal-link migration is ready; then use permanent redirects rather than breaking indexed URLs.

## Evidence boundary

Findings use current first-party UK supplier taxonomies plus HSE terminology. They establish market meaning and category clarity, not keyword demand. No paid UK search-volume, conversion or customer-interview dataset was used. Validate final H1/title wording against Search Console query data and sales-team terminology before any URL change.
